01 . The Manifesto

Stitching the broken seam of the river.

The Río Atemajac has long been a forgotten infrastructure. SUTURA proposes not a restoration in the nostalgic sense, but a radical re-stitching: a living seam that connects fragmented barrios and reintroduces native ecological succession at every scale.

"A river is not a boundary—it is the city's most generous room."
